# Evidence-bound constructive review

Bounded OpenCode R14 adaptation of K-Dense peer-review 2.2. Original source and comparison are retained in this round. Preserve LICENSE.md. This is guidance for a working assessment, not an editorial decision or a replacement for an authorized final auditor.

Use only for the requested mode and version: scientific method challenge, manuscript/proposal critique, comment-level response check or final evidence reconciliation. The primary decides whether a separate reviewer is needed. Critic stays read-only, with shell and delegation denied. No skill may expand permissions.

## Scope and confidentiality

Identify the exact manuscript/excerpt, source package, purpose and materials actually available and inspected. A public workflow fixture has no journal decision or confidential assignment; do not invent them or block it on a full formal-review intake. For a real confidential submission, establish the existing material-owner/venue authorization and applicable confidentiality, AI/tool, conflict and retention rules before dependent handling; unresolved permission is a blocker only for that material/action. Preserve originals. Never upload a confidential manuscript or review to an unapproved service or contact authors/editors without authorization.

Accountable humans retain formal reviewer and author responsibilities. Record agent inspection as such, not human endorsement. Do not infer full-text reading, raw-data access, figure inspection, reproduction or experimental validation from filenames or summaries.

## Bounded review

1. Orient to the question, system/design, unit, method and central claim. Separate public prior evidence, applicant preliminary work, current calculations and proposed future work.
2. Trace material claims to the exact source/result/figure. Check direction, magnitude, terminology, units/denominators, uncertainty, limitations and citation attribution. A valid identifier alone does not establish support. Read surrounding qualifiers before asserting a missing limitation.
3. For a consequential issue, give an ID, severity, exact location/claim, evidence status (demonstrated error, risk or missing evidence), consequence and smallest executable repair with a completion condition. A wrong number or unsupported central inference is necessary to fix; stylistic preferences and extra experiments are not automatically necessary.
4. Distinguish implementation, numerical reference agreement, empirical validity and mechanistic/causal inference. Observations/rows/frames are not automatically independent experiments. Unknown acquisition order is not a time series. Do not silently relabel measured evidence as an assumption or invent a correction.
5. Where narrowing the conclusion or correcting attribution suffices, request that. New experiments are required only if a retained claim actually depends on them. A sound bounded passage may have no required repair and no additional experiment. No mandatory number of major/minor comments or checklist score.
6. For a revision reply, compare the reply's completed-action statements with the saved revision and source package. Mark proposed/unperformed work accurately. Inspect changed claims and their dependencies; do not carry an earlier verdict over a later version.

Use current official guidance only when it applies; absent a specified venue/funder, do not impose a particular journal, clinical checklist or funding agency. Guidance supports review but never supplies absent scientific facts.

## Return and handoff

Return version and inspected scope, evidence-anchored findings, necessary actions versus optional improvements, and the unresolved gap/owner. A needed computation goes through the primary to analyst; scientific decisions to researcher; source disputes to document; prose changes to the single author. Do not write or execute those tasks yourself.

Preserve the existing authorized_review_chain and model-independence rules. This candidate imports no compulsory formal-intake JSON, Python tools, remote services, full-report scaffold, automatic skill-paper citation, or editorial outcome. Public trial self-review does not establish independent peer review; future confidential reviews still need their actual policy and human responsibilities.
